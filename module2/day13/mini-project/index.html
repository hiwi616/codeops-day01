Habesha Beans

Freshly roasted coffee, the Bole way

Habesha Beans is a cozy neighborhood cafe in Bole, Addis Ababa, serving traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremonies alongside modern espresso drinks. We source our beans directly from farmers in Yirgacheffe and Sidamo, roasting small batches fresh every morning.
